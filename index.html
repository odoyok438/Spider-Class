My recent project: Digital Humidity and Temperature Sensor

This project involved designing a digital humidity and temperature sensor using a microcontroller. I learned how to interface sensors with microcontrollers and display the readings on an LCD screen.

It was a great learning experience, and I am proud of the final product.

Components Used

  • DHT11 Humidity and Temperature Sensor
  • ESP32 Microcontroller
  • 16x2 LCD Display
Components and their jobs
Component Job
DHT11 Measures temperature and humidity
ESP32 Reads the sensor and controls the display
16x2 LCD Shows the readings

How the project works

  1. The sensor measures the humidity and temperature of the surrounding environment.
  2. The microcontroller reads the data from the sensor and processes it.
  3. The processed data is then displayed on the LCD screen.

Terms in My Project

DHT11
A low cost sensor that measures temperature and humidity.
ESP32
A microcontroller with built-in Wi-Fi and Bluetooth that reads inputs and controls outputs.
LCD
A screen that displays text and numbers.
Humidity
The amount of water vapor in the air.
Temperature
The measure of how hot or cold something is.
